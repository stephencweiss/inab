# Envelope budget rules

Date: November 4, 2026.
Status: accepted November 5, 2026. The user approved the Slice 3 design and implementation, including an earlier active start month.

The budget uses USD integer cents. Every sum, including each intermediate total, must be safe. The opening is the reviewed state entering the selected month, based on preceding posted closing balances, prior-month rollover, and explicit old pending treatment. Occurrence dates control the budget. Posting or clearing does not create a second effect. The application must review boundary pending entries, account bridges, complete elapsed assignments, and source coverage before it supplies normalized facts.

Opening held funds equal signed Cash balances plus positive Credit balances. Tracking does not supply held funds. Held funds must equal Ready to Assign plus all category Available amounts, including card reserves. Debt does not create a reserve. Ordinary opening Available amounts must be nonnegative and must already reflect prior-month rollover. Reject a negative ordinary opening with `opening-overspending-needs-resolution`. Imported negative closing Available is review evidence, not a valid direct opening seed. The application must obtain resolved opening Ready to Assign and reserve amounts with evidence; the calculator does not silently reset or classify the deficit. Negative card reserves are allowed and carry forward. Include nonzero closed Cash and Credit accounts. Each opening value requires evidence. Negative Cash or Ready to Assign needs explicit acknowledgement.

Store facts, not month-close totals. Assignments are monthly totals, not repeated deltas. Positive category funds carry forward. Cash overspending reduces the next month's Ready to Assign. Unfunded Credit spending leaves debt without payment funds. Negative payment reserves carry forward and remain visible. Future assignments are commitments; show their maximum cumulative monthly amount separately from Ready to Assign and show any funding deficit. A later return cannot fund an earlier month. Net assignments within each month before measuring this maximum. Writes must reject a new or larger funding deficit and permit repair of an existing one. Corrections can expose deficits and must show them.

Use month-wide cash priority within each spending category. A card purchase uses its positive account balance first. That portion has cash priority. Then use available category funds for debt purchases in ascending card account ID order. Net card refunds restore category funds before that allocation. Recalculate funding when the same month receives a new assignment. Category Available always includes all signed category activity. Payment reserve movement includes funded debt spending, refunds, direct assignments, and payments.

For each card and category, track the debt and positive-balance parts of reviewed purchases. Match a refund to known debt purchase amounts first, then known positive-balance purchase amounts. Treat any remaining refund as debt-related opening history. A refunded positive-balance part restores category funds without reversing a reserve; remove that part from any positive-balance Ready to Assign adjustment. No merchant or amount match is inferred. Then net debt purchases and debt-related refunds within the month before allocating funds. A net refund reverses the payment reserve, including below zero. This rule also applies when prior debt was underfunded; the deficit remains visible. A debt-reducing statement credit is not Ready to Assign. A statement credit or category refund that creates a positive card balance adds those new held funds to Ready to Assign. The category refund also restores its category and reverses its reserve. This can expose a reserve deficit after payment; do not hide it.

A card payment consumes its full amount from the reserve. When a payment crosses zero debt, its new positive card funds increase Ready to Assign. This preserves the held-funds equation without income. The [numeric examples](../budget-examples.md) define the exact results.

Process events by occurrence date, stored nonnegative order, then stable event ID. Database row order has no financial meaning. Cash priority and card funding use the full month. For a split on a positive card, apply positive account funds to gross negative allocation lines in category ID order; do not cap this amount at the net parent outflow. Adjust Ready to Assign by the positive card balance change plus positive funds spent minus refunds of known positive-funded purchases. This applies to both net inflows and net outflows. Mixed-sign splits are supported. Require an exact signed sum and one line per category.

Support Cash/Cash transfers, Cash-to-Credit payments, and Cash/Tracking boundary flows. Other directions are unsupported. Tracking boundary flows have explicit external evidence and do not create a Tracking register. Outflows require a spending category; inflows require explicit income or refund treatment. Equal opposite legs and concrete account kinds are checked at the application boundary; normalized domain transfers contain one positive amount and two distinct account IDs. One source identity has one budget effect.

Unallocated activity changes account balances but makes current and later reports incomplete. Do not describe such totals as verified. An opening amendment must pass review again. See the [Slice 3 design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-slice-3-envelope-budget-design.md) for activation, correction, and atomic storage requirements.

Reports permit at most 1,200 inclusive months from activation. Refuse a longer horizon before allocating month results. This resource limit supports up to 100 years of reviewed history.

Historical imported transfer source IDs use `history|FROM_ROW_ID|TO_ROW_ID`. Each concrete source row can be consumed once across ordinary events and both transfer legs. Validate initial paired account IDs, signed amounts, and dates against the source records. A Tracking external leg without an imported row uses `external:ACCOUNT_ID`. Plain manual evidence IDs remain available for reviewed manual bridge transfers. Store exclusive source memberships with foreign keys; preserve raw sources and audit later explicit corrections.

Reviewed signed budget account balances must also match the current register working balances. A historical bridge cannot replace missing live register activity after the register opening. History-only transfer edit/unlink cannot create or remove live legs; use a budget-only historical correction or a fully reviewed opening amendment. Material Cash/Credit adjustment source rows require budget review as well as ordinary transactions.
